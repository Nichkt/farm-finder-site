Farm Finder Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Farm Finder helps you find farms using an offline directory bundled with the app. The app does not have an account, analytics, advertising, or a server, and does not transmit your personal data to the developer.

Location

If you choose Farms near me and grant location permission, Farm Finder reads an approximate location from Android and uses it on your device to sort the bundled farm directory by distance. The location is not saved by Farm Finder or sent to the developer. You can deny or revoke location permission in Android settings and use ZIP-code, town, state, and farm-name search instead.

Saved farms and searches

Saved farms and the last search are stored in the app's local preferences. Farm Finder includes only the saved-farms preference in Android's app backup; whether Android backs it up depends on your device and backup settings. The last search and bundled farm directory are excluded from app backup. You can remove saved farms in the app or clear Farm Finder's app data in Android settings.

Links, calls, directions, and sharing

Farm Finder can open a farm's public website, dialer, email app, map app, or Android Sharesheet when you choose one of those actions. Those apps and services are governed by their own privacy policies. Farm Finder does not automatically send your location when you open a farm listing or share it.

Data in the directory

The offline directory contains farm and business listing information from USDA Local Food Directories and Eatwild. This is information about listed businesses, not information collected from app users. Directory listings can be out of date; contact farms directly to confirm details.

Children's privacy

Farm Finder is a general-audience farm directory and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change. For privacy questions, contact nichkt@gmail.com.
